# Research Questions:


1. RQ1: How accurately can OCO-2 Solar-Induced Fluorescence (SIF) be predicted from high-resolution spectral and climatological variables using machine learning, and how does the resulting enhanced SIF compare to existing state-of-the-art enhanced SIF products over Germany?

2. RQ2: How does the choice of machine learning architecture (tree-based models such as Random Forest and XGBoost versus neural network–based models such as Artificial Neural Networks and Convolutional Neural Networks), and the representation of input data (tabular features versus spatially structured CNN-compatible inputs), affect the accuracy and spatial consistency of enhanced SIF derived from OCO-2 observations?

3. RQ3: To what extent does spatial aggregation of OCO-2 supervision from native footprints to 4 km grid cells affect target stability and predictive accuracy?


4. RQ4: How does the performance of the OCO-2–based SIF enhancement model vary across different agro-climatic zones within Germany?


5. RQ5: To what extent does high-resolution, crop-pure enhanced SIF improve winter wheat yield prediction RMSE compared to mixed-pixel SIF derived from raw satellite observations?
