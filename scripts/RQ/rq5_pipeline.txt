MODEL AND DATA-PREPARATION STRUCTURE
====================================


11. RQ5 winter-wheat yield prediction pipeline
------------------------------------------------

Research question:

    To what extent does high-resolution, crop-pure enhanced SIF improve
    winter-wheat yield prediction RMSE compared with mixed-pixel SIF derived
    from raw satellite observations?

The current end-to-end order is:

    export_nuts3_regions_for_wheat_inference.R
        -> predict_monthly_winter_wheat_sif_nuts3.py
        -> extract_nuts3_winter_wheat_nirv_composition.py
        -> prepare_nuts3_raw_mixed_sif_yield_data.R
        -> attach_bayern_winter_wheat_yield.R
        -> winter-wheat-yield-crop-pure-vs-raw-sif.ipynb

The filenames in the requested list need two small corrections: monthly SIF
inference is implemented in a Python file, not an R file, and the final yield
model is currently an IPython notebook, not a plain Python script.


11.1 export_nuts3_regions_for_wheat_inference.R

Purpose:

    Defines the Bavaria NUTS3 regions used for crop-pure SIF inference and
    yield modelling.

Method:

    - Reads the Sentinel-2 raster extents for tiles 32UNA, 32UPU, 32UQV,
      32UPA and 32UPV.
    - Intersects Bavaria NUTS3 geometries with those tile extents.
    - Keeps a NUTS3 region when at least 50% of its area lies in a selected
      tile.
    - Gives the original tiles 32UNA, 32UPU and 32UQV priority when a region
      matches both an original and a newly added tile.
    - Otherwise assigns a duplicated region to the tile containing the
      largest percentage of its area.
    - Verifies that each NUTS3 region has only one assigned tile.

Principal outputs:

    data/nuts3_regions_50pct_in_mgrs.rds
    data/nuts3_regions_50pct_in_mgrs.gpkg

The GPKG is the direct spatial input to the monthly CNN inference script.


11.2 predict_monthly_winter_wheat_sif_nuts3.py

Purpose:

    Applies the trained Sentinel-2 spatial-aggregate CNN to strict
    winter-wheat pixels and summarizes the resulting enhanced SIF by NUTS3,
    year and month.

Coverage:

    Tiles:  32UNA, 32UPU, 32UQV, 32UPA and 32UPV
    Years:  2019, 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2024
    Months: March through July

Method:

    - Builds the same 20 m predictor channels and normalization used during
      CNN training.
    - Uses the annual crop-type raster to retain strict winter-wheat pixels.
    - Runs the saved spatial-aggregate CNN checkpoint in blocks rather than
      constructing a complete in-memory prediction stack.
    - Calculates the mean raw and calibrated predicted SIF over the strict
      winter-wheat pixels inside each assigned NUTS3 region.
    - Supports incremental resume at the region/scene level, so newly added
      NUTS3 regions can be processed without recomputing completed regions.

Principal outputs:

    data/winter_wheat_yield_model/monthly_crop_pure_sif/
        nuts3_monthly_strict_wheat_sif_long.csv
        nuts3_monthly_strict_wheat_sif_wide.csv
        scene_processing_log.csv

The wide table contains one row per NUTS3 region and year, with calibrated
crop-pure SIF columns for March through July.


11.3 extract_nuts3_winter_wheat_nirv_composition.py

Purpose:

    Adds regional winter-wheat composition and monthly NIRv without rerunning
    CNN inference.

Method:

    - Calculates ww_pct from the annual crop raster for each NUTS3/year.
    - Extracts monthly mean NIRv over the same strict winter-wheat support.
    - Joins these values to the completed crop-pure SIF wide table.

Principal merged output:

    data/winter_wheat_yield_model/monthly_crop_pure_sif/
        nuts3_monthly_strict_wheat_sif_nirv_wide.csv

NIRv is retained in the prepared table for diagnostics and possible future
models, although it is not used by the current final RQ5 Random Forest models.


11.4 prepare_nuts3_raw_mixed_sif_yield_data.R

Purpose:

    Builds the raw OCO-2 mixed-pixel comparison baselines and joins them to
    the crop-pure regional predictors.

Raw SIF input:

    data/extracted_modis_data/
        modis_1_12_bin_uncertainity_corrected_raw.rds

Method:

    - Keeps accepted target_modis_sif observations for the analysis years
      and March-July period.
    - Drops any existing geometry and rebuilds each OCO-2 footprint polygon
      from its four latitude/longitude corners.
    - Uses the rebuilt footprint centroid to assign each sounding to one of
      the selected NUTS3 regions.
    - Produces four raw-SIF baselines:

          raw_all:  all accepted footprints
          raw_ww05: footprints with ww_pct >= 0.05
          raw_ww10: footprints with ww_pct >= 0.10
          raw_ww30: footprints with ww_pct >= 0.30

    - Within each NUTS3/month, first averages SIF by acquisition date and
      then averages the date means. This prevents a date with many adjacent
      OCO-2 soundings from dominating a monthly value.
    - Joins the raw monthly baselines to the crop-pure SIF, NIRv and ww_pct
      table.

Principal comparison output:

    data/winter_wheat_yield_model/raw_mixed_sif/
        nuts3_crop_pure_and_raw_mixed_sif_predictors.csv

This table has one row per NUTS3 region and year and contains the crop-pure
monthly SIF predictors plus the four raw mixed-pixel SIF representations.


11.5 attach_bayern_winter_wheat_yield.R

Purpose:

    Attaches the official Bavaria winter-wheat yield target and creates the
    final RQ5 model table.

Method:

    - Combines the yearly CSV files under data/bayern_yield.
    - Reads the column "Winterweizen (einschl. Dinkel und Einkorn)".
    - Handles German text encoding and European decimal commas.
    - Converts yield from dt/ha to metric tonnes/ha by dividing by 10.
    - Joins yield to the predictor table by NUTS3 name and year.
    - Removes rows without a usable yield target.
    - Calculates one observed-month seasonal mean for each raw SIF baseline
      before imputation.
    - Estimates missing-predictor means from the training years 2019-2022
      only, then applies those fixed means to both training data and 2024.
      Thus, 2024 values do not influence imputation estimates.

Principal output:

    data/winter_wheat_yield_model/ww_model_data.csv


11.6 winter-wheat-yield-crop-pure-vs-raw-sif.ipynb

Purpose:

    Trains and evaluates the winter-wheat yield models used to answer RQ5.

Evaluation design:

    Training years: 2019-2022
    Held-out test year: 2024
    Target: ww_yield in metric tonnes/ha
    Model: RandomForestRegressor
    Fixed settings: 500 trees, max_depth = 5, min_samples_leaf = 3

The notebook fits nine candidate representations using the same Random
Forest configuration:

    - Crop-pure calibrated monthly SIF: five March-July predictors
    - Raw-all monthly SIF: five March-July predictors
    - Raw >=5% wheat monthly SIF: five March-July predictors
    - Raw >=10% wheat monthly SIF: five March-July predictors
    - Raw >=30% wheat monthly SIF: five March-July predictors
    - Four corresponding single-column raw seasonal-mean models

The current primary RQ5 comparison retains three monthly models:

    crop_pure_monthly
    raw_ww05_monthly
    raw_all_monthly

The final model predictors are the relevant SIF columns only. NIRv and ww_pct
are not included in the current final Random Forest feature sets, which keeps
the comparison focused on the SIF representation.

Principal result outputs:

    data/winter_wheat_yield_model/rq5_results/
        yield_model_performance_comparison.csv
        observed_vs_predicted_yield_models.pdf
        paired_regional_absolute_error_improvement.pdf
        bootstrap_rmse_improvement_distribution.pdf

These four outputs provide the main RQ5 evidence: overall predictive
performance, observed-versus-predicted behavior, paired regional error
changes, and uncertainty in RMSE improvement from 10,000 bootstrap draws.
