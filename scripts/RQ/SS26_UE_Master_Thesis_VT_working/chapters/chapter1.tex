\section{Outline\label{sec:outline}}

The 'structure' or 'outline' section gives a brief introduction into the main chapters of your work. Write 2-5 lines about each chapter. Usually master thesis is separated into 6-8 main chapters.
\\
\\
\noindent This example thesis is separated into 7 chapters.
\\
\\
\textbf{Chapter \ref{cha:chapter2}} analyzes the requirements for your component. This chapter provides the reader the background knowledge to understand technical concepts applied in the thesis. This chapter will have 5-10 pages.
\\
\\
\textbf{Chapter \ref{cha:chapter3}} is usually termed 'Related Work', 'State of the Art' or 'Fundamentals'. Here you will describe relevant technologies and standards related to your topic. What did other scientists propose regarding your topic? This chapter makes about 20-30 percent of the complete thesis.
\\
\\
\textbf{Chapter \ref{cha:chapter4}} is termed 'Research question'. Here you describe your Problem statement and Research questions. %This chapter will have a volume of 20-30 percent of your thesis.
\\
\\
\textbf{Chapter \ref{cha:chapter5}} describes the methodology/implementation part of your work. Don't explain every code detail but emphasize important aspects of your implementation. This chapter will have a volume of 20-30 percent of your thesis.
\\
\\
\textbf{Chapter \ref{cha:chapter6}} is Experiments, Results and Discussion. This chapter will have a volume of 15-20 percent of your thesis.
\\
\\
\textbf{Chapter \ref{cha:chapter7}} summarizes the thesis, describes the problems that occurred and gives an outlook about future work. Should have about 4-6 pages.
