\chapter{Research Questions\label{cha:chapter4}}
This chapter defines the problem addressed by the thesis and presents the research questions used to guide the SIF-enhancement and crop-yield experiments.

\section{Problem statement\label{sec:problem_statement}}
Satellite SIF provides information related to photosynthetic activity, but the sparse spatial and temporal sampling of OCO-2 limits its direct use for spatially continuous and field-scale agricultural analysis. Existing enhanced SIF products are generally available at resolutions of approximately 500~m or coarser, where observations can still contain signals from several crops and other land-cover types. It is therefore necessary to determine whether high-resolution spectral and climatological predictors can reconstruct OCO-2 SIF reliably, and how model architecture, input representation, spatial aggregation, sample density, and agro-climatic variation affect the resulting predictions. The agricultural value of this enhancement must then be assessed by testing whether crop-pure SIF improves winter wheat yield prediction and whether the resulting yield model can be transferred to another crop.

\section{Research Questions}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item[\textbf{RQ1:}] How accurately can OCO-2 Solar-Induced Fluorescence (SIF) be predicted from high-resolution spectral and climatological variables using machine learning, and how does the resulting enhanced SIF compare with existing state-of-the-art enhanced SIF products?

    \item[\textbf{RQ2:}] How do the choice of machine learning architecture (tree-based models such as Random Forest and XGBoost versus neural-network-based models such as Convolutional Neural Networks) and the representation of input data (tabular features versus spatially structured CNN-compatible inputs) affect the accuracy and spatial consistency of enhanced SIF derived from OCO-2 observations?

    \item[\textbf{RQ3:}] To what extent does spatial aggregation affect the stability and predictive accuracy of enhanced SIF derived from spatially and temporally sparse OCO-2 observations?

    \item[\textbf{RQ4:}] How does the performance of the OCO-2-based SIF enhancement model vary across different agro-climatic zones within Germany?

    \item[\textbf{RQ5:}] To what extent does high-resolution, crop-pure enhanced SIF improve winter wheat yield-prediction RMSE compared with mixed-pixel SIF derived from raw satellite observations?

    \item[\textbf{RQ6:}] How does the spatial density of OCO-2 training samples affect the uncertainty of the reconstructed SIF product at the field scale?

    \item[\textbf{RQ7:}] To what extent can the yield model trained on winter wheat be transferred to other C3 crops without loss of physiological signal integrity?
\end{enumerate}
